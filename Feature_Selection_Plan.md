# Feature Selection Plan (Tailored to our 5 Models)

Because we have strategically chosen highly advanced models (Ridge, SVR, Random Forest, LightGBM) that already possess internal regularization and built-in noise handling, a complex, multi-track feature selection "arena" is unnecessary and redundant. 

We will use a highly streamlined, two-step pipeline to prepare the dataset specifically for our 5 models.

---

## Phase 1: The Filter Method (The Safety Net)
**The Goal:** Protect the OLS (Baseline) model from completely crashing.
*   **The Math:** Pure OLS fails mathematically if two features are perfectly identical (100% correlated).
*   **The Action:** We run a Pearson Correlation scan and drop any feature with a `> 0.99` correlation score. 
*   **Status:** **COMPLETE.**

---

## Phase 2: The Embedded Selector (Lasso Regularization)
**The Goal:** Eliminate the noise generated by One-Hot Encoding (e.g., dropping categories that only have 12 houses) to speed up our SVR model and prevent Ridge from memorizing junk.
*   **The Action:** We will not run slow Wrapper methods. Instead, we will train a single **Lasso (L1) Regression** model on the dataset. 
*   **The Math:** Lasso is uniquely famous for its mathematical penalty that explicitly forces the coefficients of useless features to exactly `0.00`. 
*   **The Output:** We will look at the Lasso model's output, identify the features it crushed to zero, and permanently delete them from our dataset. We will only keep the "survivor" features that Lasso deemed mathematically important.

---

## Phase 3: The Hand-off to Notebook 04
**The Goal:** Train our official Roster.
*   **The Action:** We save this final, Lasso-distilled dataset.
*   **The Roster:** In the next notebook, we feed this exact dataset to our official 5 models:
    1. OLS Baseline
    2. Ridge Regression
    3. SVR
    4. Random Forest
    5. LightGBM
